Scope

  • 22'-1" long, 8'-0" tall horizontal privacy screen with pergola top, finished on both sides, following the reference image.
  • 7 clad posts (treated-pine core + cedar cladding) at 3'-7 1/4" on center, clear bay 3'-1 3/4". Core: treated pine 4x6, set with its 5 1/2" side across the fence, embedded 36" in Ø16" concrete footings over 6" of gravel (holes dug with a rented mini skid steer and auger). Above grade each post is clad with cedar 2x6 faces and 1" side boards, 5 1/2" wide with flush faces.
  • 24 cedar slats per bay (2x2, about 2" gaps), held in notched side boards: even spacing, no visible fasteners on the slats.
  • Pergola top: cedar 2x8 beams on both sides, bolted straight to the post cores with 4 x 1/2" thru-bolts per post (the face boards end under the beams), angled beam ends (no end caps), and 13 cedar 2x6 rafters with cloud-lift ends at each post and mid-bay, set in notches in the beams.
  • Stain & sealer on all cedar (faces, side boards, slats, beams and rafters), both sides: Ready Seal, color Natural Cedar. Details below.
  • Solid wood only, no composite; all lumber planed on 4 sides (S4S). Coated trim-head deck screws (hidden) and galvanized thru-bolts.
  • All visible parts are pre-cut, eased and finished in our shop to keep on-site work short. Cleanup and debris removal included.

  6. Slats (pre-cut)
  7. Front closure: face boards, ending under the beams
  8. Side closure (end posts)
  9. Beams (pre-notched), bolted straight to the cores (4 x 1/2" thru-bolts per post)
  10. Pergola rafters (pre-cut), posts + mid-bay
