Kitchen Privacy Screen — Proposal

Prepared for Bill · Cornelius, NC · November 10, 2026 · valid for 30 days · two options

Scope (both options)

  • 22'-1" long, 8'-0" tall horizontal privacy screen with pergola top, finished on both sides, matching the reference image.
  • Materials: solid wood only, no composite. Option A: Western Red Cedar on all visible parts. Option B: 100% pressure-treated pine. All lumber is planed on 4 sides (S4S).
  • Posts: pressure-treated pine 4x4 core (ground contact) in both options, set 24" deep in concrete over 6" of gravel, holes dug with a rented mini skid steer and auger. Above grade, each post is clad with 2x6 faces and 1" side boards (cedar in A, treated pine in B), all 5 1/2" wide with flush faces.
  • Slats: 2x4 laid flat, held in notched side boards (even spacing, no visible fasteners on the slat faces).
  • Pergola top: notched 2x8 top boards on both sides, end caps for a solid-beam look, 2x6 rafters with cloud-lift ends, one per post.
  • Fasteners: coated trim-head deck screws (small countersunk heads, rated for treated lumber) and 1/2" galvanized through-bolts.
  • All visible parts are pre-cut, notched and eased in our shop to keep on-site work short. Cleanup and debris removal included.

Comparison

Option A: cedar, golden-ratio spacing and the shortest slat span (best against warping), no stain needed. Option B: treated pine and fewer posts (lowest cost), longer slats and stain required.

Finish: all lumber in both options is S4S (surfaced/planed on 4 sides), so every piece has true, consistent dimensions and the post faces, side boards and notches line up. Rough-sawn lumber is not used. No composite material is used in either option.
Option A (cedar): Western Red Cedar, kiln-dried; if S4S cedar 2x6/2x8 is not stocked locally it will be sourced from a lumber yard. Left natural (no stain needed); weathers to silver-gray; sealer available on request.
Option B (treated pine): Southern Yellow Pine #2 Prime, pressure-treated, S4S with eased edges; ground-contact rating for the buried cores. Treated pine is delivered wet and moves as it dries; we select straight boards (kiln-dried-after-treatment when available) and apply a semi-transparent stain once it has dried.

Material prices may vary with supplier stock and availability at the time of purchase (species, grade, S4S sizes and lengths); final material cost is confirmed when the order is placed.
