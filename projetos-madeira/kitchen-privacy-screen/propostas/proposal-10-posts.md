# Proposal — Kitchen Privacy Screen (10 posts)

**Location:** Cornelius, NC · **Date:** 2026-11-10 · **Valid for:** 30 days
**Drawings:** sheets A-1, A-2, A-3 (rev. 6) — 10-post version

## Scope of work
- 22'-1" long, 8'-0" tall horizontal privacy screen with pergola top, finished on both sides.
- **10 composite posts** at 2'-4 13/16" on center (clear bay 23 5/16"; bay-to-post ratio ≈ golden ratio). Pressure-treated 4x4 ground-contact core set **24" deep** in concrete over 6" of gravel; holes dug with a rented mini skid steer and auger. Cedar cladding: 2x6 faces and 1" side boards, all 5 1/2" wide with flush faces.
- **144 cedar 2x4 slats** (16 per bay, 1 11/16" gaps) held in **notched side boards**: even spacing, no visible fasteners on the slats.
- **Pergola top:** cedar 2x8 top boards on both sides, notched to receive 10 cedar 2x6 rafters with cloud-lift ends; end caps for a solid-beam look.
- Coated trim-head deck screws (small, countersunk heads) and 1/2" through-bolts.
- All visible parts are **pre-cut and eased in our shop**, so the on-site installation is short and clean.
- Cleanup and debris removal.
