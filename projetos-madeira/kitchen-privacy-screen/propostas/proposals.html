Kitchen Privacy Screen — Proposal

Cornelius, NC · November 10, 2026 · valid for 30 days · two options

Scope (both options)

  • 22'-1" long, 8'-0" tall horizontal privacy screen with pergola top, finished on both sides, matching the reference image.
  • Posts: pressure-treated pine 4x4 core (ground contact), set 24" deep in concrete over 6" of gravel, holes dug with a rented mini skid steer and auger. Above grade, each post is clad in cedar: 2x6 faces and 1" side boards, all 5 1/2" wide with flush faces. The buried part is treated pine only, for lower cost and longer life in the soil.
  • Slats: cedar 2x4 laid flat, held in notched side boards (even spacing, no visible fasteners on the slat faces).
  • Pergola top: notched cedar 2x8 top boards on both sides, end caps for a solid-beam look, cedar 2x6 rafters with cloud-lift ends, one per post.
  • Fasteners: coated trim-head deck screws (small countersunk heads, rated for treated lumber) and 1/2" galvanized through-bolts.
  • All visible parts are pre-cut, notched and eased in our shop to keep on-site work short. Cleanup and debris removal included.

Finish: all cedar is Western Red Cedar, kiln-dried and S4S (surfaced/planed on 4 sides), so every piece has true, consistent dimensions and the post faces, side boards and notches line up. Rough-sawn cedar (as stocked at some big-box stores in 2x6/2x8) is not used; if S4S is not stocked locally, it will be sourced from a lumber yard. Cedar is left natural (no stain needed) and will weather to silver-gray; sealer available on request.
